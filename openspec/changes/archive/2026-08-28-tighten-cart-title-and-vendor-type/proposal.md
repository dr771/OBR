## Why

The cart drawer needs a tighter product-text rhythm: a smaller vendor label and a more compact multi-line product title.

## What Changes

- Set the cart vendor caption to 0.9rem.
- Reduce cart product-name line height to 1.9rem.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `cart-drawer-line-item-layout`: update product-name line height.

## Impact

- `assets/component-cart-drawer.css` only.
