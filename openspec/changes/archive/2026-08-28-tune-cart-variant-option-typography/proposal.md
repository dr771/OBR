## Why

The cart's variant line is currently visually lighter and less integrated than the product name. Matching its size while tightening tracking improves the compact line-item hierarchy.

## What Changes

- Set `.product-option.cart-item__variant-options` to the cart product-name font size.
- Set its letter spacing to `-0.4px`.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `cart-drawer-line-item-layout`: document the variant-option typography.

## Impact

- `assets/component-cart-drawer.css` only.
