# Blog

The old site's blog carries about a quarter of all search clicks (12,464 of 42.7k in 16 months), so
it is recreated in Shopify. **Built on dev 2026-10-05**: blog `inspiratie` (title "Blog"), 14 posts,
linked from the footer's "Over ons" column. The redirects for it are generated by
`scripts/build-redirects.js`; see [SEO-REDIRECTS.md](SEO-REDIRECTS.md).

## URL structure (owner decision 2026-10-01)

Shopify fixes the shape as `/blogs/<blog handle>/<post handle>`. A post directly under `/blogs/` is
not possible. Drupal used `/nl/blog/<category>/<post>`: 19 posts in 14 categories.

- **One blog; the old categories become tags.** Working handle `inspiratie`. If the blog is created
  under another handle, change `BLOG` in `scripts/build-redirects.js`.
- **Post handle = the old last slug segment, unchanged**, so each redirect is mechanical:
  `/nl/blog/hielspoor/fitflop-d-slipper-…` → `/blogs/inspiratie/fitflop-d-slipper-…`.
- **Tag = the old category slug**: `/nl/blog/hielspoor` → `/blogs/inspiratie/tagged/hielspoor`.
  `/nl/blog` → `/blogs/inspiratie`.
- **Blog-like pages that lived outside `/blog/`** become posts under the same rule.

Why not one blog per Drupal category (`/blogs/hielspoor/…`): 14 blogs for 19 posts, 11 of them with
a single post; Shopify has no page listing posts across blogs; every new topic would need a new
blog; and there is no SEO gain, since every post needs a redirect either way.

## What was built (2026-10-05)

- **Blog:** handle `inspiratie` (confirmed by the owner), title "Blog", comments closed.
- **14 posts**, handles unchanged, text/SEO title/meta description 1:1 from the old site. The
  payloads are in [blog/articles.json](blog/articles.json) (`ArticleCreateInput` without `blogId`),
  so the live shop can be filled from the same file. The bodies were diffed against the live dev
  storefront after creation: identical.
- **Tags are the old category labels** (`Hielspoor`, `Schoenen op vakantie`, `FitFlop`, …). Shopify
  handleizes a tag for its URL, so the label gives `/tagged/hielspoor`, the old slug. A renamed tag
  breaks its redirect.
- **Not recreated (8), redirected to `/blogs/inspiratie` in `redirects/overrides.csv`:** the six
  posts about brands no longer sold (3 × Buffalo, Dune London, Sledgers, Scholl), `wintersolden-original-brands`
  (an expired sale post built on Dockers/Buffalo/Dune prices) and
  `black-week-tot-en-met-30-korting-op-de-nieuwe-collectie`. The Scholl and Black Week posts are
  unpublished on the old site (403), so their text is not available anyway. The 13 tag pages left
  without a post have the same override.
- **Links inside the posts** point at the new shop: old paths went through the redirect map, old
  product links without a match go to the brand collection, `/nl/zoeken?s=x` became `/search?q=x`.
- **Images:** 65 files in Shopify Files, named `blog-NN-…`, taken from the old site's originals.
  Post bodies reference them by CDN URL, so they are dev-shop URLs (see MIGRATION-TO-LIVE.md).
- **Publish dates** are not shown on the old site. Each post got the first of the month of its
  image upload folder (`/2018-03/` → 2018-03-01) so the order is roughly right; the theme hides dates.
- **`microwobbleboard`** was a product-selection page with two paragraphs of text, not an article.
  It is a short post now, with a link to the FitFlop collection. If its 666 clicks should land on
  products instead, make it a collection and put an override in `redirects/overrides.csv`.
- **Theme:** `sections/main-blog.liquid` (tinted hero, tags as pills, card grid),
  `sections/main-article.liquid`, `assets/component-ob-blog.css` (holds the body markup contract).
